# Tanasub Open Code of Conduct

This community welcomes writers, researchers, and contributors across experience levels, languages, cultures, and identities. This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project-managed community spaces.

## Expected behavior

- Treat people with respect. Critique ideas, evidence, and writing choices without attacking the person.
- Give specific, constructive feedback and remain open to correction.
- Respect cultural and linguistic differences; do not treat one tradition as a universal norm.
- Protect privacy and credit others' work accurately.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, sexual harassment, doxxing, and repeated disruptive behavior are unacceptable. Do not publish someone's private information or drafts without permission.

## Reporting and enforcement

Use GitHub's **Report content** option, when available, on an abusive issue, comment, or pull request without posting sensitive details publicly. If the option is unavailable, use the GitHub Support contact form linked in [GitHub's reporting instructions](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam). This reports abuse to GitHub; direct reporting to repository admins depends on repository settings. For an ordinary moderation request that contains no sensitive information, ask a repository maintainer in the relevant thread. Report security vulnerabilities through the [security policy](.github/SECURITY.md).

Maintainers may edit or remove disruptive content, close or lock conversations, issue warnings, or restrict participation using GitHub's moderation tools. Responses should be proportionate to the behavior and its impact. Disagreements about craft or evidence alone are not misconduct.
