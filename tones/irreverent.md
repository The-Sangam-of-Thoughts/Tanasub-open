# Writing in the Irreverent Tone

## 1 · Definition and Boundaries

Irreverent is the narrator's voice crossing a sacred or self-important register — ceremony, office, piety-as-performance, expertise — and collapsing the status gap in the moment instead of arguing against it. The tone owns texture, never stakes; the license must be legible to *this* audience before the first crossing begins.

**Recognition test.** Underline each elevated register the passage crosses and each license marker that authorizes it.
- No elevated register named → this is snark or a joke, not irreverence.
- No license marker legible (persona, frame, genre convention, program slot) → the crossing is unlicensed; see §7 mode 2.
- Delete the deflation: if the paragraph does the same work, the crossing was decoration [@clm-tone-irreverent-deflation-expectation].

**Boundaries, as observable voice-level claims.**
- **vs `tone-comedic`.** Comedic is carried by content and structure — setup, turn, payoff — and may need no target at all; irreverence needs something currently elevated to lower, and the collapse itself is the beat. Test: point to the elevated register a joke can name none.
- **vs `tone-deadpan`.** Deadpan is *localized* flatness in which understatement is the vehicle and the surface claims nothing; irreverence claims a crossing. Remove the elevated register: deadpan still works, irreverence goes inert.
- **vs `tone-whimsical`.** Whimsical delights in invention with no joke machinery and no butt; irreverence invents nothing — it levels whatever is currently elevated. Whimsy builds, irreverence lowers.
- **vs `satirical-literature` (mode).** Irreverent voice levels whichever register is elevated *here*, so its sustained butt rotates or stays generic (authority-in-general, piety-in-general) [@clm-tone-irreverent-no-sustained-target]. That is a claim about voice across a piece, not a ban on recurring foils: a straight-man antagonist may return without a program of critique (the Nasreddin cycle fixes Timur as foil — recorded counterevidence). One identified target *plus* a critique is the mode's engine, not this tone's.
- **vs `tone-cynical`.** Cynical distrust never returns to benevolence; irreverence teases from inside a shared frame and can still like what it lowers.
- **vs `tone-defiant`.** Defiance names a power, a stake, and a warrant and resists; irreverence mocks and needs none of the three [@clm-tone-irreverent-hierarchy-leveling].

## 2 · Why It Works — The Mechanism

The chain: an elevated register is built up or crossed, the crossing appraises as safe, the status gap collapses in the moment, and the reader — positioned as an insider — completes the forbidden step themselves. Evidence honesty first: no experimental study isolates "irreverent prose tone" as a variable (evidence-incomplete, dossier §B), so every mechanism below is scoped to a verified adjacent finding or a named tradition.

**Collapse, not counter-argument (moderate).** Kant locates laughter in a built-up solemn expectation collapsing into nothing — English renderings of §54 differ, so the section is paraphrased and never quoted [@clm-tone-irreverent-deflation-expectation] [@src-irreverent-kant-critique-judgment-1790].
- Craft consequence: the collapse *is* the beat; a rebuttal, a sneer adverb, or a label ("ridiculous") spends it.
- Scope: any passage that escalates ceremonial diction before dropping it. If the buildup was never felt as solemn, the collapse produces nothing [@clm-tone-irreverent-taboo-register-breach].

**The crossing must appraise as safe (moderate).** Across five experiments amusement followed violations that were simultaneously appraised benign, via three routes — an alternative norm, weak commitment to the violated norm, psychological distance [@clm-tone-irreverent-benign-violation-register] [@src-irreverent-mcgraw-warren-2010].
- Craft consequence: irreverence is a two-part machine — a genuine crossing *plus* a frame that keeps it safe — so benignity is engineered, not assumed.
- Counterevidence held: negativity dominance means a failed appraisal can outweigh the comic gain and stick — once a deflation reads as real contempt, that reading dominates the amusement [@src-irreverent-rozin-royzman-2001].

**Charge is borrowed from the prohibition (moderate).** Taboo words are defined and sanctioned by institutions of power and reiterated in child-rearing, so a register's breach takes its energy from whoever polices it [@clm-tone-irreverent-taboo-register-breach] [@src-irreverent-jay-taboo-2009]; Freud's whole-work economy adds that forbidden material supplies energy the joke saves [@src-irreverent-freud-jokes-1905].
- Craft consequence: aim at registers this audience still protects. Counter-side recorded: habitual breach means a dead prohibition yields no charge.

**Leveling is felt, not argued (strong).** Irreverence collapses a status or sanctity gap in the moment — kyōgen's servant embarrassing an arrogant master inside its sanctioned slot, Kabir's verses lowering priestly garb [@clm-tone-irreverent-hierarchy-leveling].
- Held-open counterevidence: Hobbes's "sudden glory" makes laughter an assertion of superiority [@src-irreverent-hobbes-leviathan-1651], so the same deflation can *enact* rank instead of leveling it — the frame decides which reading arrives.
- Scope: a felt moment inside a comic frame; no evidence reviewed shows literary deflation changing real power relations.

**License first; reader as co-conspirator (strong / moderate).** Insult is permitted because the relationship — not the individuals — authorizes it [@src-irreverent-radcliffe-brown-joking-1940] [@clm-tone-irreverent-licensed-frame], and the voice then reads from alongside the reader, so moralizing mid-deflation converts conspiracy into prosecution [@clm-tone-irreverent-co-conspirator-distance].
- Limits kept labeled: Raskin is whole-work only (chapter pages unverified) [@src-irreverent-raskin-ssth-1985]; Provine is Tier 4, book-level [@src-irreverent-provine-laughter-2000]; no prose-satiation study exists — habituation rests on Berlyne at whole-work scope, confidence **limited** [@clm-tone-irreverent-dosage-habituation].

## 3 · When It Works

**By form.**

| Form | What the tone does | Caution |
|---|---|---|
| academic | Deflates a field's self-important framing in one illustrative sentence, then returns to the argument | Yield at thesis, definitions, and every data claim; irony cannot be promised to persuade |
| blog/marketing | Anti-pompous candor names the category's existing hype before the claim does | Yield at the CTA; never aim the mockery at the reader's own group |
| fiction | Voices a narrator who lowers whatever register the scene elevates; gives affectionate distance from offices and institutions | The engine owns stakes — never deflate the danger, the bond, or the grief |
| poetry | One deflation per movement against ritual or ceremony; concrete signs do the indicting | Density kills cadence; keep the deflation aimed at clerical show, never at the devotion itself |
| copy | One deflating qualifier can carry a slogan against category self-importance | Product, safety, medical, and legal lines stay literal |

**By audience state.**
- The reader must register the crossing as real *and* the prohibition as still felt — breach of a dead taboo is just vocabulary [@clm-tone-irreverent-taboo-register-breach].
- The reader must already stand outside the register: shared distance is the benignity condition the voice supplies [@clm-tone-irreverent-co-conspirator-distance].
- The license must be legible to *this* audience before the first deflation [@clm-tone-irreverent-licensed-frame].
- Weakest where the reader holds the register as lived sacred without irony, or identifies with the butt as a category of people [@clm-tone-irreverent-prejudiced-norm-backfire].

**By position.**
- **Openings:** strong — one licensed deflation fixes the contract; never two in a row before the frame is established.
- **Middles:** strong as rotation, weak as filler — each beat should lower a *different* elevated register [@clm-tone-irreverent-no-sustained-target].
- **Endings:** yield — close on consequence, affection, or plain speech; a final sneer converts co-conspirators into spectators.

**By purpose.**
- **Entertain:** native. **Teach:** compatible when the deflation is the illustration, never the claim.
- **Persuade:** weak — mockery collapses a status gap, it does not supply an argument.
- **Move:** incompatible at the emotional peak; *Mort* models the yield (§4) [@work-mort-1987].

## 4 · When to Show Restraint

Blocking guidance. **Honest null:** no study isolates irreverent-prose tone or its saturation as a variable (dossier §B, evidence-incomplete). Every count below is **writer-inference** — observable signals from exemplary practice plus adjacent findings (Berlyne's satiation at whole-work scope [@src-irreverent-berlyne-aesthetics-1971]; eight status studies of norm-violating humor [@src-irreverent-bitterly-status-2017]) — with confidence **limited** on frequency [@clm-tone-irreverent-dosage-habituation] and **strong, source-stated** on the ethical line [@clm-tone-irreverent-prejudiced-norm-backfire]. "Use sparingly" is a review-blocker; the counts make the rule checkable.

**Dosage threshold (observable; writer-inference, confidence limited).**
- Long-form prose: no more than **one sustained deflating passage per ~1,200 words** unless each one lowers a *different* elevated register than the last.
- **Never two consecutive deflations without a neutral beat between** — a failed appraisal can outweigh the comic gain and stick, so pace rather than escalate.
- **Three or more beats on the same target type** → change target, cadence, or register before repeating a deflation that already landed.
- Short forms (epigram, comic scene, one post) may run denser: they end before habituation bites.
- Serial or returning-reader work re-earns the license each entry; a reader who files the last installment as "the joke column" has already habituated.

**Saturation signals — reader, then draft.**
- Reader: predicts the next deflation, skims it, quotes the voice back as a joke, or asks "is this meant to be serious?"
- Draft: (a) two or more consecutive deflations with no neutral beat; (b) three-plus beats on one target type; (c) an identical solemn-setup/banal-collapse repeated verbatim; (d) a moral gloss following the laugh; (e) a sneer with no license marker legible to this audience.

**Context prohibitions.** Crisis, safety, medical, or legal communication where the reader must act literally; bereavement and live-grief messages; any statement of a harm the reader is currently suffering; a subject this audience holds sacred without irony. There the benign frame cannot be established and the crossing converts to contempt.

**Ethical bounds — the bright line.** Aim at registers and offices, never at people: humor aimed at a *group* signals a norm tolerating discrimination, and in two experiments hostile sexism predicted donations cut from a women's organization after sexist jokes and skits but not after nonhumorous statements of the same content [@clm-tone-irreverent-prejudiced-norm-backfire] [@src-irreverent-ford-ferguson-2004] [@src-irreverent-ford-sexist-humor-2008].
- Diagnostic: if the butt is a category of people rather than an elevated register, the tone has already failed — and may enforce the hierarchy it seemed to mock.

**Where to drop it deliberately.** Stakes peaks and grief — *Mort* moves to pathos at its peak instead of sermonizing [@work-mort-1987] (critical context bibliographic only [@src-irreverent-butler-guilty-2001]); genuine deadlines (`tone-urgent`); a subject the audience holds sacred (`tone-reverent`); any passage where a critique has both a target and a program (satirical-literature).

**Substitutions:** One plain, concrete sentence at the peak; pathos where sincerity is demanded; the institution's own record where a critique is really wanted; plain exposition between deflations.

## 5 · How to Use It — Operators

Nine constructions carry the tone; each is built, never asserted (dossier §D).

| Operator | Construction | Why it lands |
|---|---|---|
| Solemn buildup, banal collapse | Escalate ceremonial diction, answer it with the mundane; the collapse is the beat | Laughter sits in the expectation collapsing into nothing [@clm-tone-irreverent-deflation-expectation] |
| Deadpan reference-register | Deliver absurdity in the voice of a manual, guide, or entry | Faux-authority supplies distance and makes the reader a co-conspirator [@clm-tone-irreverent-co-conspirator-distance] — the in-world Guide device in *Hitchhiker's Guide* is an official voice whose officialness licenses the deflation, described from secondary confirmation [@work-hitchhikers-guide-1979] [@src-irreverent-sfe-entries] |
| Straight-faced personification | Give an office or cosmic function a household, a job description, and social habits | Awe falls into domesticity with nobody mocking aloud [@clm-tone-irreverent-hierarchy-leveling] — *Mort*'s Death as well-meaning civil servant [@work-mort-1987] |
| Material/bodily debasement | Answer dignity with appetite and body inside a legible carnival frame | The lofty is dragged into material terms for a sanctioned duration [@clm-tone-irreverent-carnival-debasement] — Rabelais's giants against knightly and clerical pomp, Bakhtin-mediated, chapter locators unverified [@work-gargantua-pantagruel] |
| Concrete outward signs as evidence | Catalog garments, beads, threads, and rituals; let material detail indict performance | Doctrine is never argued; the sign contradicts the claim [@clm-tone-irreverent-indian-anticlerical-hasya] |
| Rule-bound insolence | License the mockery through a formal rule with an expiry — riddle-game, role pair, fixed slot | Permission is structural and known to end [@clm-tone-irreverent-licensed-frame] |
| Register whiplash in dialogue | Authority speaks procedural politeness while the situation is insane | The mismatch mocks without a sneering narrator |
| The withheld moral gloss | Do not explain or moralize after the deflation | The reader completes the forbidden step themselves |
| Rotating targets, no program | Mock whatever register is elevated *there* — office, piety, expertise | No single butt accumulates [@clm-tone-irreverent-no-sustained-target] |

**Lexicon fields.**

| Carry the tone | Avoid |
|---|---|
| Office and ceremony nouns: the office, the chair, the see, the rite, the protocol | Sneer adverbs and intensifiers: obviously, predictably, inevitably, utterly |
| Material detail as evidence: robe, bead, thread, plaque, gown, collection plate | Group nouns used as the butt: them, those people, that lot |
| Procedure verbs delivered earnestly: convened, certified, sanctioned, ordained | Abstract verdicts as narration: vile, contemptible, beneath dignity |
| Plain domestic nouns set against grand subjects | Moral gloss after the laugh: which is why, the truth is, we must |

**Syntax patterns.**
- Stack subordinate or periodic clauses in the buildup, then land the collapse in a short flat main clause; the gap carries the laugh [@clm-tone-irreverent-deflation-expectation].
- In dialogue keep the authority's syntax impeccably procedural; never let the narrator supply the raised eyebrow.
- Withhold the purpose-clause after the deflation ("because…") — that is exactly where the sermon would go.

**Rhythm.** Long buildup, short landing; one pause before the collapse, never three — over-pausing advertises the sneer. Neutral sentences between deflations are load-bearing: they reset arousal so the next crossing still registers [@clm-tone-irreverent-dosage-habituation].

**Imagery.** Ceremonial objects at close range — robes, lecterns, nameplates, forms, ritual implements — answered by domestic or bodily fact. The image must be picturable and must belong to the elevated register it betrays.

**Psychic distance.** Middle distance by default: the narrator stands beside the reader, never above the target's humanity [@clm-tone-irreverent-co-conspirator-distance]. Go wide when the register is unfamiliar — one neutral setup beat first supplies the distance; go close only inside a viewpoint the text can test.

**Figurative moves.**

| Move | Use it for | Watch |
|---|---|---|
| Mock-formal inflation | Treating trivia with full ceremonial diction | Inflation must serve the collapse, never decorate |
| Litotes / meiosis | Letting the landing undersell the buildup | Understatement is the vehicle, never the license |
| Analogy down | Explaining the sacred in household or bureaucratic terms | It must lower the register, not the people in it |

**Before → after pairs (original prose; each isolates one operator).**

1. *Solemn buildup, banal collapse.*
   - Before: "The board met to discuss the merger."
   - After: "The board assembled in conclave. Its first resolution moved the biscuits to the far end of the table."
   - Isolation: ceremonial diction builds, the mundane landing collapses it, and no comment follows.

2. *Straight-faced personification of an office.*
   - Before: "The tax authority is an intimidating bureaucracy."
   - After: "The tax authority was a widower of about sixty, particular about tea, and unable to hold a gaze past the second syllable."
   - Isolation: the abstraction gets a household and habits; awe falls into domesticity with nobody sneering.

3. *Concrete outward signs as evidence.*
   - Before: "The monastery's piety was performative."
   - After: "The gate issued a robe with each visitor, a rosary with each robe, and a receipt for the lot."
   - Isolation: material detail alone indicts the performance — no doctrine argued, no person named.

4. *Register whiplash in dialogue.*
   - Before: "The officials were unhelpful while the building burned."
   - After: "You're quite right to be on fire, said the duty officer, but the desk is engaged until four. May I suggest the corridor?"
   - Isolation: procedural politeness against an insane situation does the mocking; the narrator adds nothing.

## 6 · Combination Rules

Family: Humor. On stakes the genre wins, on texture the tone wins, on vocabulary the register wins; route one primary tone only. Patterns are named from [`_combination-rules.md`](_combination-rules.md).

| Genre family | Pattern | Engine property that must survive |
|---|---|---|
| Speculative | re-color | The premise's "what if?" and the world's rules survive; irreverence deflates in-world pomp (officials, rites, rank), never the premise itself |
| Horror | re-color | Dread, threat, and the payoff survive; mockery may meet a character's pomp or an institution's reassurance, never the danger — drop the voice at the scare |
| Romance | re-color | The optimistic arc and the bond's emotional risk survive; deflect obstacles and officious third parties, and yield at the confession |
| Thriller & Mystery | re-color | Jeopardy, the clock, and fair play survive [@clm-reader-promise]; no clue and no threat may be deflated into harmless |
| Literary & Realist | integrate (novel with irreverent narration) | Genre-true: character truth and consequence; tone-true: rotating register deflation that never turns a character into a prop (§7 mode 1 scope) |
| Dramatic & Screen | re-color | Scene turns and dialogue subtext survive [@clm-scene-turning-points] [@clm-dialogue-subtext]; insolence rides role-pairs inside the scene — kyōgen's method — and never stalls it, so a comic line serves subtext rather than becoming a talking head |
| Poetic & Lyric | alternate | Lyric music and, in devotional lyric, sincere devotion survive; lyric dwelling and the comic beat trade dominance by section, joined by a bridge, and where the lineage is native — bhakti and vachana verse — leveling may hold the foreground; the deflation aims at clerical show, never at the devotion itself [@clm-tone-irreverent-indian-anticlerical-hasya] |
| Structural & Traditional | re-color | Form rules survive (epistle, frame, flash, dastan, katha); the frame supplies the license — a riddle-cycle's built-in expiry keeps insolence rule-bound [@work-vikram-vampire-1870] |
| Academic (Layer 0) | re-color | Argument, evidence, and the thesis survive; one deflating illustration per claim, never in place of it |
| Blog/marketing (Layer 0) | re-color | The promised takeaway and the CTA stay literal; irreverent deflates the category's buzzwordery, yields at the CTA, and never mocks the reader's problem or the client's promise, with the reader's own group outside the mockery |

Cross-cutting: satirical-literature is a mode, not a family — where a piece must prosecute one identified target through structure, the mode owns the critique and this tone supplies only texture. Dominance rule: a tone may never silently override the engine the reader was promised. A pairing that fits none of the three patterns is flagged **high-tension** and resolved explicitly, not forced.

## 7 · Failure Modes and Revision

Four modes in the repository's standard sequence; every example is original prose.

**1 · Punching down (aiming at people)**
- **Mechanism:** disparagement aimed at a *group* implies a norm tolerating discrimination and, in hostile audiences, released the discriminatory behavior that nonhumorous statements of the same content did not release [@clm-tone-irreverent-prejudiced-norm-backfire].
- **Example:** a running gag mocks how "those people" talk; no office, rite, or register appears anywhere in the scene.
- **Effect:** amusement collapses into offence, and the piece enforces the hierarchy it seemed to mock.
- **Diagnostic:** name the butt in three words. A category of people rather than an elevated register = fail.
- **Revision action:** re-aim at the office, the rite, the procedure, or the institution's performance — never at the people inside it.
- **Scope:** the documented effect concerns group-directed disparagement under laboratory exposure; register-directed mockery, self-deprecation, in-group affiliation, and a mode's chosen named target sit outside it.

**2 · Unlicensed breach (snark without a frame)**
- **Mechanism:** the crossing happens before any license is legible, so audiences police it as failure rather than daring [@src-irreverent-bell-failed-humor-2015]; a failed benignity appraises as contempt, and negativity dominance magnifies and retains that reading [@clm-tone-irreverent-benign-violation-register] [@clm-tone-irreverent-licensed-frame].
- **Example:** three paragraphs of sneer at a denomination with no persona, no comic frame, and no genre convention a reader could point to.
- **Effect:** the reader reads contempt rather than tease, distrusts the voice, and stops granting the next crossing the benefit of the doubt.
- **Diagnostic:** point to the license marker legible to *this* audience — persona, frame, program slot, genre convention. Cannot point = unlicensed.
- **Revision action:** establish the license first (a persona beat, a neutral setup that creates distance, or a form that grants the slot), or cut the passage.
- **Scope:** an earned persona carries portable license — the curmudgeon, the guide-voice — but misuse revokes it.

**3 · Aggressive drift (escalating the same gesture)**
- **Mechanism:** chronic deflation-without-license is the aggressive humor style, and repeated out-of-key violation compounds status cost instead of being forgiven as a running gag [@clm-tone-irreverent-aggressive-style-drift].
- **Example:** a serial piece where each installment strains for a bigger version of the same insolence toward the same kind of target.
- **Effect:** the voice reads as mean rather than merry; readers skip the asides and quote them back as "the joke column".
- **Diagnostic:** count beats by target type — three-plus on one type, or an identical solemn-setup/banal-collapse repeated verbatim, is over threshold (§4).
- **Revision action:** pace, don't escalate — change target, cadence, or register, and insert a neutral beat before the next deflation.
- **Scope:** trait-level, correlational evidence [@src-irreverent-martin-humor-styles-2003]; running gags that *vary* the setup are variation, not repetition.

**4 · Neighbor creep and the moral gloss**
- **Mechanism:** the voice slides to a neighbor — satire-creep (one fixed target plus an argument), cynicism-creep (distance that never returns to benevolence), grandstanding-creep (a moral gloss after the laugh turns conspiracy into prosecution) [@clm-tone-irreverent-co-conspirator-distance].
- **Example:** a draft lands a deflation, then stops to explain in the author's own voice why pomp is really a moral failing.
- **Effect:** the reader gets a lecture instead of a co-conspiracy; the shared stance collapses on the spot.
- **Diagnostic:** three checks — is there a program of critique against one identified target? does the voice still like anything? does a moral gloss follow the laugh? Drift tests: if a built setup–turn–payoff with an inflected reacting surface has appeared, the tone has become tone-comedic — route to its card or cut the punchline; if the surface has gone flat and the deflation machinery stopped, tone-deadpan; if invention accrues with no violated expectation, tone-whimsical; if the voice names a power, a stake, and a warrant and resists, tone-defiant.
- **Revision action:** cut the gloss and let the reader complete the step; hand the piece to the satirical mode if the program is real; restore one line of warmth if the voice likes nothing.
- **Scope:** Juvenalian directness and a declared curmudgeon persona are conventions, not failures, where the form has always spoken that way. Where the subject is someone's lived sacred — grief, ritual that matters to a participant — it is never convention: deflation registers as contempt rather than teasing, so re-earn the license or yield the register (§4).

## 8 · Cultural Scope

The attitude is neither Anglophone nor secular: five traditions carry it with *different* license sources, so no false universal of "irreverence" is asserted [@clm-tone-irreverent-indian-anticlerical-hasya].

**India (main analysis) — Kabir, devotion as license.** The 1915 English *One Hundred Poems of Kabir* is the fully verified anchor: the introduction frames the tradition's apparatus of piety as what Kabir denounced (p. xvi), and the verses mock priestly costume, rosary, idol, and ritual bath (XXI pp. 28–29; LXV–LXVII pp. 68–71; LXIX pp. 72–73) — described, never quoted [@work-kabir-hundred-poems-1915] [@src-irreverent-kabir-tagore-1915].
- Load-bearing point: the ridicule is voiced *from inside devotion* — sacred feeling licenses the anticlerical sting — which is precisely why the register is irreverent rather than cynical.
- **Translation limit:** every locator refers to the 1915 English verse; the source language behind the Tagore–Underhill mediation is unrecorded (left null rather than guessed), so claims about Kabir's sound and idiom stay bounded.
- **Rasa correspondence, not equivalence:** hasya is the nearest classical category via shared [@clm-rasa-emotional-aesthetic]; no Sanskrit "irreverent rasa" is claimed, and the *Natyashastra* hasya chapter locator (commonly cited as ch. 6) was not verified this session — evidence-incomplete [@src-natyashastra-ghosh-1951].
- Secondary Indian frame: the Vikram–Betaal riddle-cycle licenses impertinence toward a sovereign through rules with an expiry — frame verified (twenty-five attempts), tales uninspected, analysis partial [@work-vikram-vampire-1870] [@src-irreverent-burton-vikram-1870].
- **Dropped and labeled:** the Birbal anecdote tradition and any peer-reviewed source for Mulla-Nasruddin-in-India — no scholarly source located, so neither is used as evidence (evidence-incomplete).

## 9 · Exercises

**1 — Rotate the target (operator: rotating targets; §7 mode 3).**
- Write a 200-word passage deflating three *different* elevated registers — one ceremonial, one professional, one institutional — with a neutral sentence between each.
- **Success criteria:** a peer can name three distinct registers; no two beats share a target type; a neutral beat separates every pair; nothing reads as a program against one butt.

**2 — Engineer the license (failure mode: unlicensed breach).**
- Take a sneering passage from your draft and rewrite it twice: once with a legible license marker in the first sentence (persona, frame, genre convention, program slot), once with a neutral setup beat that creates distance before the crossing.
- **Success criteria:** you can name the marker in three words; a beta reader reports "she's teasing, not sneering"; deleting the marker returns the passage to §7 mode 2.

**3 — Yield at the peak (operator: withheld moral gloss; §4).**
- Deflate a solemn register, then version the stakes-peak three ways: the deflation running over the peak, a moral gloss after the laugh, and a plain or pathos line in place of the deflation.
- **Success criteria:** version three keeps the stakes intact (the *Mort* yield); in version two you can name the exact sentence that converts conspiracy into prosecution; version one displays a §4 saturation signal.
