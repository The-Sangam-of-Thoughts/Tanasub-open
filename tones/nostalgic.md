# Writing in the Nostalgic Tone

## 1 · Definition and Boundaries

The nostalgic voice speaks from a remembering now about a remembered (or imagined) then, holding fondness and absence at once — longing for a specific past colored bittersweet, never purely positive [@clm-tone-nostalgic-bittersweet-structure]. It is past-anchored and dual-timed: the craft signature is controlled shuttling between then and now, not an undated glow [@clm-tone-nostalgic-dual-time-distance]. Tone owns texture, not stakes.

**Recognition test (the tense/temporal test).** Underline every past-tense run and every present-frame sentence. The passage is nostalgic only if (a) both poles are on the page — a located now speaking about a located then — and (b) both valences sound at once: fondness *and* the ache of distance. Past-only narration is plain reminiscence; fondness with no absence is `tone-warm`; a present loss intruding on now with no fond distance is `tone-elegiac`.

**Boundary with `tone-elegiac`:** the elegiac voice is present-tense grief with tenderness and requires no past orientation; nostalgia requires the finished then. Test: "Is the loss still owed, still happening?" — yes, elegiac; "Were those days good, and are they over?" — yes, nostalgic. The alias table routes the bare word "bittersweet" to `tone-elegiac` with nostalgic secondary (see [`tones/_index.md`](_index.md)).

**Boundary with `tone-reflective`:** the reflective voice turns inward on personal experience and keeps the past under present assessment — no temporal idealization; the nostalgic voice idealizes and longs. One remembered scene serves either: if the now-self *judges* the then, reflective; if it *misses* it, nostalgic. Evaluation absent, longing present → this card.

**What it is not.** Not a diary of yearning — unanchored ache with no located then is restlessness, not this tone. Not a program for return — that is restorative nostalgia, §7. Not another register's job: active grief belongs to `tone-elegiac`, affectionate presence to `tone-warm`, the worked question to `tone-reflective`. On stakes the genre wins; on texture this tone wins.

## 2 · Why It Works — The Mechanism

No experiment isolates "nostalgic prose tone" as a variable — an honest null the dossier records rather than papers over. Each mechanism below is scoped to a verified adjacent finding or a named literary tradition, with theory, author, and locator.

**Mixed valence (bittersweet, not purely positive) — strong.** Content and trigger analyses (Wildschut et al. 2006, *JPSP* 91(5):975–993, Studies 1–4) found nostalgic accounts more positive than negative yet systematically containing negative feelings, with sad, poignant, lonely events as frequent triggers; the controlling definition — self-conscious, bittersweet but predominantly positive and fundamentally social, fond memories mixed with yearning — is Sedikides & Wildschut's "To Nostalgize" review (*AESP* 51:189–273); pancultural prototype ratings across 18 countries (Hepper et al. 2014, *Emotion* 14(4):733–747) rank pleasant features above unpleasant everywhere while the unpleasant stay central [@clm-tone-nostalgic-bittersweet-structure]. Craft consequence: the bitter is load-bearing — pure cheer collapses into `tone-warm`, pure grief into `tone-elegiac`. Batcho (2013, *History of Psychology* 16(3):165–176) adds the concept's own history, from medical disease to psychological construct, as the duality written into the word itself.

**Self-continuity and social belonging — strong.** A four-study causal chain (Sedikides et al. 2015, *EJSP* 45(1):52–61) shows negative self-discontinuity heightens nostalgia and induced nostalgia augments self-continuity beyond positive affect; six experiments (Sedikides et al. 2016, *Emotion* 16(4):524–539) and Routledge et al. (2011, *JPSP* 101(3):638–652, Studies 1–6) show connectedness is the pathway producing self-continuity and meaning in life; induced nostalgic recollections carry more first-person-plural pronouns and social words (Wildschut 2006, Studies 5–7) [@clm-tone-nostalgic-social-cast] [@clm-tone-nostalgic-self-continuity-frame]. Craft consequence: populate the remembered world with people and make the narrating self answerable to the remembered self — an empty chair works only by implying the absent other.

**Triggers: present lack or concrete sensory reminder — strong.** Induced loneliness increases nostalgia, only *negative* self-discontinuity evokes it, and meaning threats increase it (Wildschut 2006, Study 4; Sedikides et al. 2015, Studies 1–2; Routledge et al. 2011, Studies 3–4) [@clm-tone-nostalgic-dysphoria-trigger]. Craft consequence: give the character a present-tense deficit or a scene-level cue on the page — longing with no current lack reads as decoration.

**Rosy retrospection — moderate.** Across three event studies, recollection was more positive than the lived experience, negative in-event thoughts fading within days (Mitchell et al. 1997, *JESP* 33(4):421–448) [@clm-tone-nostalgic-rosey-retrospection]. Memory revises upward: idealization is licensable when focalized to the remembering narrator, invisible — and therefore propagandistic — when it drifts into unmarked narration (§7).

**Two registers — moderate (literary-critical, tier 3).** Boym's *The Future of Nostalgia* (2001, Introduction) distinguishes *restorative* nostalgia — present-focused, rebuilding the lost home as truth, unaware it is nostalgia at all — from *reflective* nostalgia — dwelling in ambivalence and the drawn-out texture of longing, aware the past is unreachable [@clm-tone-nostalgic-restorative-reflective]. Reflective sustains the tone; restorative hardens into ideology.

**Chosen vs ambient — moderate.** Daily-diary measurement found spontaneous state nostalgia covarying with rumination and sadness while experimentally induced recollections rate more positive — a method difference, not a contradiction (Newman et al. 2020, *JPSP* 118(2):325–347, Studies 2–5); anticipatory nostalgia tilts toward worry (Batcho 2020, *Frontiers in Psychology* 11:1186) [@clm-tone-nostalgic-mixed-valence-dailylife]. Craft consequence: bound each nostalgic flight by a scene-level cue; never run it as ambient weather.

**Dual-time frame — moderate (craft inference from psychology + exemplars).** Every feature of nostalgia is past-anchored while the feeling is appraised now (*Emotion Review*, doi 10.1177/17540739241303497): two positions by definition [@clm-tone-nostalgic-dual-time-distance]. The dossier's exemplars — Proust's narrator seized by recollection mid-act [@work-swanns-way], Kalidasa's messenger holding present separation and remembered intimacy in one address [@work-meghaduta] — demonstrate the frame by different machinery; described, never quoted.

**Honest nulls.** No study measures nostalgic *prose* dosage (every §4 count is writer-inference scoped from the diary and induction findings), and no verified source maps this tone onto a codified rasa (§8 records that as evidence-incomplete).

## 3 · When It Works

**By form.**

| Form | What the tone does | Caution |
|---|---|---|
| Academic | frames motivation and lineage — why this question matters to this inquirer | era-praise never substitutes for warrant; the evidence contract owns |
| Blog / marketing | anniversary and throwback content readers recognize as their own past | must clear the §7 consumption-ethics gate |
| Fiction | scene textured by a remembering now; memory structure may carry plot | must yield at action peaks (§4 counts) |
| Poetry | the two-position turn in a stanza-group; the ordinary object opening the then | unbroken reverie saturates — anchor each flight |
| Copy | recognition and warmth aimed at an audience's real lived past | never brand-bound manufactured longing (§7) |
| Dramatic / screen | prop, held beat, and image under dialogue carry the then | a reverie speech at the action peak kills urgency |
| Structural / traditional | frame commentary, dated entries, letters home | the form's unit survives; nostalgia sits in the frame |

**By audience state.** The reader must accept the then as specific and worth attending, grant the narrator standing to remember (a felt relationship to the material), and — for idealization to convert — receive the past as focalized memory rather than asserted fact [@clm-tone-nostalgic-rosey-retrospection]. The tone fails on a reader primed for urgency or adjudication; a reader in present grief may receive raw ache rather than bittersweet reward (writer-inference, not a measured reader-side effect).

**By position.**

| Position | Use | Caution |
|---|---|---|
| Opening | one concrete trigger opening the then | never an undated glow — establish the now first |
| Middle | the one dominating nostalgic passage per section (§4) | yield to scene at the genre's stakes-peak |
| Ending | bittersweet close holding both valences, or return to a changed present | no golden-age moral, no unmarked uplift |
| Transitions | bridge in and out via a concrete event or object | two competing reveries in one unit: cut or cool one |

**By purpose.**

| Purpose | Compatibility | Caution |
|---|---|---|
| teach | compatible as motivation and framing | never as warrant — the evidence owns the claim |
| persuade | works on a real shared past the audience lived | fails ethically on an invented brand-bound past (§4, §7) |
| move | native — the tone's home purpose | dosage rules (§4) still bind it |
| entertain | compatible as texture | the engine must still own the payoff |

**By contract.** Diary, letter, and frame forms license longer flights because the form itself promises recollection; open marketplace copy gets the shortest leash — every flight must clear the §4 counts and the §7 ethics gate.

**By length.** In a short piece one flight may be the whole; in extended work density must fall as length rises, and the §4 counts govern.

## 4 · When to Show Restraint

**Blocking thresholds (counts, not vibes).** No prose-dosage study exists; every number below is writer-inference bounded from the dossier's diary/induction findings and exemplars (§H) [@clm-tone-nostalgic-mixed-valence-dailylife]. "Use sparingly" is a review-blocker; cite the counts below instead.

- **One dominating nostalgic passage per scene or section.** A second in the same unit: keep the stronger, cut the other or cool it to counter-register.
- **A counter-register within about one page (~400–600 words) of concentrated reverie** — a present-tense fact (work, weather, task, another voice). This is the chosen-vs-ambient boundary made countable.
- **At most two stacked sensory-detail clusters before returning to the present scene.** A third in sequence is saturation, not escalation.
- **Puncture rule: the remembered past must be complicated at least once per sustained passage** — a sour note, a wrong detail, someone who was not actually happy. Uncorrected upward revision is §7 sepia-wash [@clm-tone-nostalgic-rosey-retrospection].
- **Every flight enters through a concrete scene-level cue** (taste, route, object, name). No cue anywhere in a long passage means ambient nostalgia — the rumination profile.
- **Exception:** a short lyric or one-voice frame may sustain a single unbroken movement; the thresholds govern extended prose and sequences.

**Saturation signals (observable, dossier §H6).** (a) three or more consecutive past-tense sentences with no present-frame anchor or narrator response; (b) trigger objects accumulating with no character attached; (c) longing adjectives ("miss," "simpler times," "golden") doing work the two-position structure should do; (d) unqualified era-praise in narration; (e) the passage survives deletion of its present frame entirely — then it is reminiscence, not nostalgic tone; (f) no scene-level cue anywhere in a long passage. Reader side: skimming, distrust, fatigue — detect in the draft by marking clusters, counter-registers, and punctures.

**Context prohibitions.** Not over active-threat or urgent-action content — it kills urgency (high-tension pairing, §6). Not over active grief with no fond distance, where `tone-elegiac` owns the material. Not in safety, legal, or medical copy, where a looking-back voice reads as unreliability. Not as unexamined era-praise in historical or political material — that is restorative hardening (§7).

**Ethical bounds.** Who is harmed: readers engineered into attachment to a shared past they never had. Measured reason: nostalgia weakens the desire for money and loosens spending across six experiments (Lasaleta et al. 2014, *JCR* 41(3):713–729) — exactly why advertising runs on it [@clm-tone-nostalgic-consumption-ethics]. The line: prose serving the reader's felt past is craft; brand-bound invented shared pasts are manipulation, not craft.

**Where to drop it deliberately:** at the genre's stakes-peak — the chase, the revelation, the union beat, the argument's core claim. **Substitutions:** at the peak, plain present-tense declarative fact; scene and action; task-detail with no emotion vocabulary — then return to the passage with a bridge.

**Signal table.**

| Signal | Threshold | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Sensory clusters in sequence | more than 2 | return to the present scene |
| Pages of concentrated reverie | no counter-register within ~1 page | insert a present-tense fact |
| Competing reveries | more than one dominating passage per section | keep one, cool the other |
| Golden then | no puncture anywhere in the passage | add one complicating fact |
| Longing adjectives | a label where the two-position structure should work | rebuild the frame, delete the label |
| Cueing | flight entered with no scene-level trigger | add the concrete cue at entry |

## 5 · How to Use It — Operators

**Lexicon fields.**

| Carry | Avoid |
|---|---|
| Specific remembered nouns: the tin whistle, Bell Street, her mother's second kitchen | Era-labels doing feeling-work: "the good old days," "simpler times," "golden age" |
| Verbs of return and recurrence: came back, still stood, every August | Longing adjectives stacked as load-bearers: miss, wistful, halcyon on the noun |
| Concrete triggers — taste, route, weather, a carried object; ordinary over monumental | Universalizing evasions: "we all remember," "everyone my age recalls" |
| Named people and household pronouns (we, they, you) in the remembered world | Scenery alone — trigger objects with nobody attached to them |
| Focalization markers: I thought then, it seemed, from here | Unqualified era-praise stated as narration's fact |

**Tense/frame structure.** The remembered scene runs in past tense inside a present-frame sentence at entry and at least once per page — the then/now shuttle is the tone's audible signature [@clm-tone-nostalgic-dual-time-distance]. The frame carries the ache; delete it and the passage becomes plain reminiscence (§4e).

**Sensory detail.** One concrete trigger opens the past [@clm-tone-nostalgic-dysphoria-trigger], then three or four particulars of the remembered world — ordinary objects over monuments. Detail must implicate a person; objects alone saturate [@clm-tone-nostalgic-social-cast].

**Rhythm and sentence length.** Dwelling cadence: a long accumulating sentence (catalogue of particulars) followed by a short present-frame fact — the drop out of reverie. The pace difference *is* the structure; unrelieved long sentences read as dream-blur (§7).

**Psychic distance.** Near to the remembered self — its ignorance, its small satisfactions — then a measured step back to the narrating self who answers for the memory [@clm-tone-nostalgic-self-continuity-frame], using distance as a cognition tool (shared [@clm-point-of-view-psychic-distance]). Held near only, the passage immerses without ache; held far only, it summarizes without longing.

**Figuration.** Similes that compare *across* time (then against now); litotes and understatement of absence; marked personification of the past only as the narrator's figure. Focalized idealization ("I thought it enormous") beats asserted perfection every time [@clm-tone-nostalgic-rosey-retrospection].

**Original before → after pairs.**

1. *Operator: two-position frame (isolate: tense/frame structure).*
   Before: "Those summers were the best of times — endless, golden, ours."
   After: "In July we were certain the summer would never end. I stand at the same corner now, thirty years on, and the certainty arrives with me; the timetable on the wall says otherwise."

2. *Operator: concrete trigger replacing era-label (isolate: lexicon + trigger).*
   Before: "I miss the old neighborhood and the simpler times we shared there."
   After: "The grocer on Bell Street has closed, but his brass scale still sits in the doorway, and every time I pass it I am nine again, putting down more pennies than my mother sent me with."

3. *Operator: puncture complicating the golden then (isolate: focalized idealization).*
   Before: "Our street was a perfect place where everyone looked after one another."
   After: "On our street everyone looked after one another — sugar lent across fences, gates mended before asking — though nobody ever said why Mrs. Okonkwo's eldest left in such a hurry, or where he went."

**Named operators.**

- **Frame sandwich:** present fact → past scene → present fact; the ache lives in the returns, not in the glow.
- **Puncture:** one detail that refuses the golden reading — the sour note inside the sweetness [@clm-tone-nostalgic-rosey-retrospection].
- **Absent other:** name or imply the person the object is really about — the empty chair only works by implying who sat there [@clm-tone-nostalgic-social-cast].

**Diagnostic pass.** Delete every era-label and longing adjective from a reverie block. If the passage still aches, the structure is working; if it goes flat, the longing was declared rather than composed — rebuild from trigger, people, and frame.

## 6 · Combination Rules

Patterns are named from [`_combination-rules.md`](_combination-rules.md): re-color, alternate, integrate. On stakes the genre wins; on texture the tone wins; on vocabulary the register wins.

| Genre family | Pattern | Engine property that survives |
|---|---|---|
| Speculative | alternate | Cognitive estrangement and wonder survive; nostalgia owns the lull after the reveal and backstory only — never the reveal itself |
| Horror | re-color | Dread and the active threat survive; a corrupted or lost past may haunt the approach or coda, never defuse the scare |
| Romance | integrate (bittersweet) | Emotional risk and the tradition's ending survive; both invariants: the union beat stays genre-true, the then stays double-valenced — HEA-bound drafts that canonize one golden moment alternate instead |
| Thriller & Mystery | alternate | Jeopardy survives; the return-to-hometown or cold-case reverie owns backstory and deduction lulls only, never the active threat (§4) |
| Literary & Realist | integrate (nostalgic historical fiction) | Interiority and social detail survive; the tone's most native prose home — [historical-fiction](../genres/historical-fiction.md), [bildungsroman](../subgenres/bildungsroman.md), [frame-narrative](../genres/frame-narrative.md) |
| Dramatic & Screen | re-color | Scene action and blocking survive; the then lives in prop, subtext, and held beat, never in a speech that stops the scene |
| Poetic & Lyric | integrate (reverie lyric) | Lyric music and suggestion survive; one reverie movement per stanza-group with a present anchor — [ghazal-sequence](../genres/ghazal-sequence.md) and [pastoral](../genres/pastoral-literature.md) carry remembrance as heritage |
| Structural & Traditional | alternate | The form's unit survives (entry, letter, nested tale); nostalgia sits in frame commentary and interludes — [epistolary-fiction](../genres/epistolary-fiction.md), [katha-tradition](../genres/katha-tradition.md) |
| Academic (Layer 0) | re-color | The evidence contract and bounded conclusion survive; nostalgia appears only as documented framing — motivation, acknowledgment of a lineage — never as warrant |
| Blog / marketing (Layer 0) | re-color | The reader promise and scannable structure survive; throwback content must clear §4 counts and the §7 consumption-ethics gate |

**High-tension resolution.** Nostalgic × active-threat or urgent-action content is flagged high-tension: resolve by alternating — one flight at rest points only, with the §4 counts — or avoid.

**Mode note (satirical-literature, a mode not a family).** Golden-age thinking is satire's standing target: the mode adopts this voice as the object being punctured and keeps the mic; the narrator's era-praise becomes the joke's straight man, never the piece's stance.

**Modulation, not drift.** Within the Contemplation family, moving from nostalgia into reflective analysis or elegy requires a bridge and a reason (typically the concrete event that demands the shift). Diagnostic: can you name the tone of each section? If not, the draft drifted rather than modulated.

## 7 · Failure Modes and Revision

Four modes in the repository's six-part format; each scope line marks where the pattern is convention rather than failure.

**Sepia-wash (oneiric blur — rosy retrospection uncorrected)**
- **Mechanism:** memory revises upward (Mitchell et al. 1997) [@clm-tone-nostalgic-rosey-retrospection]; when idealization drifts from focalized memory into unmarked fact, the golden then becomes asserted reality.
- **Example:** three paragraphs in which the old town is uniformly warm, every neighbor kind, no friction and no "I thought then" marking the lens.
- **Effect:** the reader distrusts the picture; prose reads gauzy and dream-blurred, and where the era is historical, propaganda for an era that never was.
- **Diagnostic:** unqualified era-praise in narration; deleting the focalization markers changes nothing; no puncture anywhere in the passage (§4).
- **Revision action:** restore the narrating frame ("I thought," "from here"), add one complicating fact to the then, and mark one detail as the narrator's present coloring.
- **Scope:** mythic, fairy-tale, and children's registers run flat-golden by contract, and a brief lyric flight under the §4 exception is exempt.

**Misery-porn / dysphoria spiral (ambient longing run as weather)**
- **Mechanism:** spontaneous daily-life nostalgia covaries with rumination and sadness, and anticipatory nostalgia blocks enjoying the present (Newman et al. 2020; Batcho 2020) [@clm-tone-nostalgic-mixed-valence-dailylife]; a passage with no scene-level cue and no return reproduces the ambient profile, not the chosen one.
- **Example:** section after section of loss-stacking — everything was better, everything is gone — with no cue, no counter-register, no people, only deprivation detail.
- **Effect:** the reader numbs and skims; longing curdles into an exhibition of ache, and the piece performs yearning instead of composing it — drifting toward the elegiac/dysphoria register (§1 neighbor crossing).
- **Diagnostic:** no scene-level cue anywhere; three-plus consecutive past-tense sentences with no anchor; the same loss restated across two paragraphs with no new specificity.
- **Revision action:** attach the concrete trigger, insert the counter-register within the page, and give the loss one specific person plus one act of the present.
- **Scope:** trauma narratives and depressive first person may deliberately model ambient ache when the narration marks the cycling as the character's condition — then it is characterization, not tone failure.

**Nostalgia bait (consumerist manipulation)**
- **Mechanism:** nostalgia weakens the desire for money and loosens spending (Lasaleta et al. 2014, six experiments) [@clm-tone-nostalgic-consumption-ethics]; copy that engineers attachment to a brand-bound shared past the audience never had is persuasion wearing craft's clothes.
- **Example:** a throwback campaign assembling stock "childhood" imagery around a product and claiming "the summers you remember" for buyers forty years apart.
- **Effect:** readers who lived that past feel their memory cheapened; those who did not feel excluded — either way the longing was manufactured for conversion, and shared [@clm-melodrama-unearned-emotion] applies.
- **Diagnostic:** name the shared past the piece invokes — is it the reader's own lived past or a past assembled for the seller? Does a product sit where a person should be?
- **Revision action:** ground the longing in a real, specific, non-commercial shared event, or cut the nostalgia and state the present benefit plainly.
- **Scope:** invoking a real shared event a real audience lived through stays on the craft side, and historical fiction reconstructing an era the reader never lived in serves imagination when it gives truth-conditions (period accuracy, disclosed framing) rather than a curated void.

**Restorative hardening / stasis (toward ideology)**
- **Mechanism:** restorative nostalgia denies being nostalgia at all and rebuilds the lost home as truth (Boym 2001) [@clm-tone-nostalgic-restorative-reflective]; on the character level, self-continuity calcifies into an identity with no room for change — the Gatsby dark mirror (Ward, *NYT* 2018, tier 5) [@clm-tone-nostalgic-self-continuity-frame] [@work-the-great-gatsby].
- **Example:** an invented small town sealed off from history — no disagreement, no dating, no decay — narrated as how things should still be.
- **Effect:** the world reads as refusal of the present; longing becomes program, and readers experience golden-age politics instead of texture.
- **Diagnostic:** does anything in the remembered world date, decay, or disagree? Can the narrating self be wrong about the then? Naipaul's stasis critique of Narayan's Malgudi, relayed by Nicholl (2011, tier 5), names the fiction-level test [@src-nostalgic-nicholl-2011-rereading-narayan].
- **Revision action:** date the world, give it friction and a dissenting voice, let the narrating self answer back — keep the dwelling, drop the program.
- **Scope:** fable, parable, and pastoral convention-fields legitimately run outside history when the frame marks the exemption; the stasis critique of Malgudi itself is a live critical controversy, not a settled verdict.

Boundary test before approving any nostalgic run: two temporal positions plus double valence define the tone (§1); one dominating passage, one puncture, and one counter-register within the page keep it inside dosage (§4).

## 8 · Cultural Scope

The dossier's survey establishes family resemblance, not a universal idiom: across 18 countries (*N* = 1,704) samples agreed on the rank-order of 35 nostalgia features, yet cluster analyses revealed country-group variation and all samples except the African ones shared the same factor structure [@clm-tone-nostalgic-crosscultural-nonuniform] [@src-nostalgic-hepper-2014-pancultural]. Shared core, no uniform voice — the traditions below are main analysis.

**Indian / Sanskrit (main analysis).** Kalidasa's *Meghadūta* (c. 4th–5th c. CE; trans. Ryder, Dent Everyman's 1912 — described, never quoted) is a classical lyric of sustained separation-longing: an exiled yaksha asks a passing cloud to carry his message to his wife, and the route across the subcontinent runs through named landmarks and seasons while present separation, remembered intimacy, and hoped-for arrival are held in one address [@clm-tone-nostalgic-dual-time-distance] [@src-nostalgic-kalidasa-meghaduta-ryder] [@work-meghaduta]. It is evidence that an Indian literary practice of longing-at-a-distance predates the European term by more than a millennium [@clm-tone-nostalgic-crosscultural-nonuniform]. Modern Indian English supplies a second case: Narayan's *Malgudi Days* (1943) builds an invented town from ordinary daily texture, and readers describe rereading as return to a place they know — while Naipaul's stasis critique, relayed by Nicholl (2011, tier 5), reads the same town as fable unchanged by history [@work-malgudi-days] [@src-nostalgic-nicholl-2011-rereading-narayan]. Reflective dwelling versus restorative stasis in one canon: the controversy is live and this card does not settle it.

**Evidence-incomplete (rasa mapping).** The repository holds no verified source mapping this tone onto a codified Indian rasa. The *Natyashastra*'s sentiment system and Anandavardhana's *dhvani* suggestion theory are neighboring frames only (shared, read-only: [@src-natyashastra-ghosh-1951] [@src-anandavardhana-dhvanyaloka-1990] [@clm-rasa-emotional-aesthetic] [@clm-dhvani-suggestion-theory]); the mapping itself is recorded as evidence-incomplete, not asserted.

**Japanese (main analysis).** Three strata weight the mixture differently. Everyday *natsukashii* — warm and gratitude-framed, from *natsuku*, of keeping something close until fondness for it grows — indicates joy and gratitude for the past rather than a desire to return to it (BBC Travel, Hobart 2020 — journalism, tier 5, corroborating only, never sole support; gloss paraphrased) [@src-nostalgic-hobart-2020-natsukashii]. *Furusato* ("hometown") nostalgia is an institutionally steered idiom: Robertson (1988) documents postwar politicians, planners, and advertisers mobilizing it, with *furusato-zukuri* described as a political project blending nativism and nationalism [@src-nostalgic-robertson-1988-furusato]. And enka's longing imagery has been shaped by commercial forces since the 1970s (Yano 2002, Harvard UP) [@src-nostalgic-yano-2002-tears-of-longing]. The local weighting is simultaneously linguistic and political.

**Portuguese (main analysis).** *Saudade*: Silva (2012, *Emotion Review* 4(2):203–211) argues it is a key Portuguese emotion whose prototypical object is something good from the past, which can also target the *future* (*saudades do futuro*) — overwhelming, long-lasting, positively evaluated; Neto & Mullet (2014, *JCCP* 45(4):660–670) map how laypersons in Portugal actually conceptualize it as a locally structured emotion concept, not a translation of Anglo "nostalgia" [@src-nostalgic-silva-2012-saudade] [@src-nostalgic-neto-mullet-2014-saudade].

**Translation limits.** English "nostalgia" cannot carry *saudade*'s future-targeting time-structure or *natsukashii*'s gratitude-without-ache; rendering all of them as "nostalgia" erases exactly the weighting differences the cross-cultural claim records. A writer borrowing these traditions translates the *structure* — distance, object, social cast — and never drops the foreign word in as ornament.

**No false universal.** Sanskrit longing rides messenger and landscape; Japanese registers split warm (*natsukashii*) from political (*furusato*); Portuguese *saudade* carries its own clock. Past-anchored, social, and bittersweet are family-resemblance features shared across traditions — not one idiom, and not a license to naturalize any single tradition's voice as "the" nostalgic one [@clm-tone-nostalgic-crosscultural-nonuniform].

## 9 · Exercises

Each exercise drills a named §5 operator or a §7 diagnostic and ends in an explicit success criterion.

**Exercise 1 — Two-position rebuild (practises the tense/frame operator).** Take ~80 words of undated golden-age summary and rebuild it: a present-frame sentence at entry, the remembered scene in past tense, a present-frame fact before the close. *Success criteria:* both temporal positions are audible without a date; deleting the present frame visibly damages the passage; no era-label survives [@clm-tone-nostalgic-dual-time-distance].

**Exercise 2 — Puncture and counter-register audit (practises §4 counts against §7 sepia-wash).** Mark sensory clusters, counter-registers, and punctures across a reverie block in your draft. *Success criteria:* no more than two sensory clusters stand in sequence; a present-tense counter-register appears within the page; at least one fact complicates the remembered past; zero unqualified era-praise statements remain [@clm-tone-nostalgic-rosey-retrospection] [@clm-tone-nostalgic-mixed-valence-dailylife].

**Exercise 3 — Populate the remembered world (practises the social-cast operator).** Rewrite a scenery-only memory passage so that a person is named, quoted, or implied inside every object-cluster. *Success criteria:* at least one other person appears per reverie — an absence counts only when the text makes the absent other felt in the object — and deleting the objects still leaves a relationship readable [@clm-tone-nostalgic-social-cast].
