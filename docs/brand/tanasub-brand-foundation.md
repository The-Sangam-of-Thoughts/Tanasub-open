# Tanasub Brand Foundation

Status: initial naming foundation
Applies to: Tanasub product family and Tanasub Open public repository

## Name

**Tanasub** is pronounced approximately *tuh-NAA-sub*. The name is drawn from the Urdu word تناسب and evokes proportion, harmony, relation, and meaningful correspondence. It fits a writing system designed to discover which ideas belong together, reject forced connections, and carry a coherent relationship through a finished work.

Use **Tanasub** for the product family. Do not expand it as an acronym.

## Product names

| Name | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Tanasub** | The product family and umbrella brand |
| **Tanasub Open** | The public repository, craft library, local baseline, schemas, validators, adapters, and examples |
| **Tanasub Studio** | The future hosted writing experience |
| **Research-to-Resonance** | The named method that turns researched observations into coherent creative architecture |

Use the full product name on first mention. “Tanasub” may stand alone after the relevant product has been established. Avoid informal variants such as “Tanasub AI,” “Tanasub OSS,” or “Tanasub Pro” unless they are formally adopted later.

## Positioning

**Category:** AI craft layer for deliberate, high-quality writing.

**Core promise:** Tanasub helps a writer move from an intention to a coherent piece by researching the material, discovering meaningful relationships, testing whether those relationships hold, and shaping the strongest ones into form.

**Short line:** Writing through meaningful connections.

Tanasub should not be presented as a generic text generator, an authorship-disguise tool, or a collection of prompt tricks. Its distinction is the quality of the writing process and the judgments made between research and composition.

## Scope

Tanasub Open is self-contained: it remains useful without a hosted account, a network connection, or any external service.

## Voice

Tanasub speaks with thoughtful precision. It is literary without ornament for ornament’s sake, technically credible without sounding mechanical, and confident only where evidence or demonstrated craft warrants confidence.

Prefer concrete mechanisms, examples, trade-offs, and observable effects. Avoid inflated claims such as “revolutionary,” generic promises of “human-like” writing, and claims that research alone guarantees quality. Let examples and comparative evaluation establish the difference.

## Compatibility

The existing machine-facing skill identifier `universal-writing-guide` remains stable during the rebrand. Paths, schema identifiers, package names, and platform references should be renamed only through an explicit compatibility and migration decision. Visible headings and product prose should use Tanasub Open now.

## Visual identity status

The name, product architecture, positioning, and verbal voice are established here. Logo, typography, color, iconography, and motion rules remain intentionally unselected. Do not invent or imply approved visual assets before that work is completed.
