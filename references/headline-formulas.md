# Headline working reference

Draft several candidates, then choose the clearest truthful promise for the reader and format. These are editing heuristics, not guaranteed performance formulas.

| Structure | Template | Use when |
|---|---|---|
| Direct benefit | `[Outcome] without [credible constraint]` | The result and tradeoff are known. |
| How-to | `How to [result] when [specific condition]` | The piece teaches a real process. |
| Specific list | `[Number] [items] for [reader/use]` | The body contains distinct scannable items. |
| Answerable question | `[Question the body resolves]?` | Reader intent naturally takes question form. |
| Finding or tension | `[Supported result], but [consequence or constraint]` | Evidence establishes a useful contradiction. |

## Review scorecard

AWAI documents the Four U's as useful, urgent, unique, and ultra-specific. Treat each as a question, not a requirement:

- **Useful:** Does the reader gain something they came for?
- **Urgent:** Is there an honest time or consequence reason to act? A durable guide may need none.
- **Unique:** Does the angle distinguish itself through actual content?
- **Ultra-specific:** Can vague nouns become a concrete audience, outcome, object, or condition?
- **Fidelity:** Does the body fully support the headline's promise?

Reject clickbait, false scarcity, fear without evidence, and claims the body cannot deliver. Platform length limits may require compression, but never loss of fidelity.
