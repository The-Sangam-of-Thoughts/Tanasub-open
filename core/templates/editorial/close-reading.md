# Close Reading: {{preferred_label}}

## Selection and access

Identify the work ID, creator, period role, language, region, form or medium, edition/version, translator where applicable, access conditions, and why this work is substantially different from the package's other close readings. Do not reproduce copyrighted full text.

## Reading scope and method

Define the passage, scene, sequence, stanza, episode, interaction, or other unit examined using a lawful locator. State the research questions and distinguish observation, paraphrase, synthesis, and researcher inference.

## Context without plot-summary substitution

Give only the context required to understand the mechanics. A plot summary cannot stand in for analysis.

## Mechanics analysis

Analyse premise, reader promise, opening, point of view or functional equivalent, character system, conflict and stakes, structure, scene/unit movement, dialogue or equivalent, setting, style and devices, emotional progression, suspense or anticipation, climax or culmination, ending, conventions, innovation, and subversion. Explain every inapplicable field and analyse its functional equivalent.

## Technique-to-effect evidence

For each important observation, record the technique, intended or documented effect, claim ID, supporting source/work ID and locator, expression type, scope, exception, confidence, and counterreading.

## Indian, regional, tradition, and translation context

Address culturally and historically specific practices on their own terms. For translated work, separate source-language verification from effects observed in the consulted translation and identify the translator/edition limitations.

## Screenwriting or adaptation context

When the work is a script or adaptation, attribute only textual or writing-level mechanics to the writer. Mark performance, direction, editing, design, music, and production evidence separately. If screen/adaptation is irrelevant, state the relevance decision.

## Reception check

Compare the reading with professional criticism and, separately, audience response. Neither category automatically proves authorial intent or general reader effect.

## Practitioner transfer

Derive scoped craft lessons, counterexamples, diagnostic questions, revision applications, and an accessibility consideration. Do not generalize beyond the evidence.

## Cross-links and limitations

Link to the primary-work record, claim matrix, relevant mandatory files, comparison and reception analyses, and sources. State unresolved disagreement and evidence limitations.

## Acceptance record

For every reading, provide the work/axis classification evidence, exact consulted edition and language, selected unit locator, and verification-event IDs. Across the three readings, record creator diversity and at least two additional applicable diversity dimensions (for example period, language, region, medium, tradition, or form). A reading with an unsupported work/axis relationship, inaccessible unit, or missing technique-to-effect chain is incomplete and cannot be used as a positive control for package completion.
