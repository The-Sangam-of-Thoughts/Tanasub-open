# Reader Promise and Emotional Engine: {{preferred_label}}

## Audience and evidence scope

Identify intended and documented audiences, cultures, languages, periods, media, and evidence types. Keep professional criticism distinct from reader or audience response.

## Reader promise

State the experiences the category conventionally offers and which are required, optional, contested, or market-dependent. Tie claims to claim IDs and evidence IDs.

## Emotional sequence

Map anticipation, identification or distance, pleasure, discomfort, surprise, tension, release, and satisfaction. Explain variation and counterexamples rather than prescribing one universal sequence.

## Mechanisms

Connect emotion to premise, structure, character or functional equivalent, voice, setting, pacing, devices, climax, and ending. Link to the corresponding analysis files.

## Cultural, Indian, regional, and translation variation

Analyse evidenced differences in expectations and affect. Do not treat audience response as proof of authorial intent or assume translated reception reproduces source-language effects.

## Screen and adaptation variation

Where relevant, distinguish writing choices from acting, direction, music, editing, or marketing. Compare reader, viewer, listener, participant, or live-audience promises only on supported dimensions.

## Failure diagnostics and revision

For each broken promise, record observable symptom, likely cause, diagnostic question, repair strategy, and risk of overcorrection. Include accessibility and content-warning considerations.

## Evidence, limitations, and cross-links

List support, counterevidence, scope, exceptions, confidence, evidence limitations, and links to definition, pacing, conventions, reception, and revision files.
