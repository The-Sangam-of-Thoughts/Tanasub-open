# Exercise: {{preferred_label}}

## Skill and rationale

Name one observable craft skill, the linked claim IDs and evidence IDs, applicable scope, exceptions, confidence, and the failure mode this exercise addresses.

## Level, form, and context

State difficulty, estimated working session, form or medium, intended audience, language and cultural context, and prerequisites. Explain the functional equivalent when a standard story element is inapplicable.

## Materials and copyright boundary

List lawful materials and locators. Use only brief, necessary reference excerpts or paraphrase; never reproduce a copyrighted work, screenplay, or large passage.

## Constraints

Give clear compositional constraints that isolate the target skill. Identify Indian, regional, tradition, translation, screenwriting, or adaptation context where relevant without forcing false equivalence.

## Steps

Provide numbered actions from observation and planning through drafting, diagnosis, revision, and evidence-aware reflection.

## Completion evidence

Define what the writer produces and what an independent reviewer can inspect. Effort or word count alone is not evidence of skill.

## Self-diagnostic and revision

Ask questions tied to observable outcomes; map each weak result to a specific revision move and warn against overcorrection.

## Accessibility alternative

Offer an equivalent way to complete the same skill objective for relevant visual, auditory, motor, cognitive, language, or technology barriers.

## Cross-links

Link to the teaching section, failure diagnostic, relevant close reading, claim matrix, and sources. Keep professional reception evidence separate from audience response.

## Acceptance check

Before use, identify the observable output, success criterion, reflection question, extension, and accessibility alternative that preserves the same skill objective. A word count, completion claim, or generic prompt is not a success criterion. If the linked claim is limited, disputed, untranslated, or inapplicable to the writer's stated context, disclose that limit and provide a bounded alternative.
