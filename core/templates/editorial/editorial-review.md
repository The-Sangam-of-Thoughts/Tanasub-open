# Independent Editorial Review

## Independence and scope

Confirm the reviewer did not author the reviewed artifacts. List the package version, files reviewed, evidence records sampled, tests consulted, exclusions, and review date. This document records review findings; it does not substitute for a human gate decision.

## Mandatory completeness

Verify the main guide, files `01` through `15`, three substantially different close readings, neighbour comparison, reception analysis, genre map, exercises, and all required cross-links. Every inapplicable topic must explain why and provide a functional equivalent.

## Teachability and practical utility

Assess whether guidance is axis-specific, ordered, actionable, supported by examples, connected to diagnostics and revision, and usable from idea through independent review.

## Evidence fidelity

Sample consequential claims against claim, source, and work IDs. Verify quotations, paraphrases, synthesis, and researcher inferences are distinguished; counterevidence, scope, exceptions, confidence, limitations, access, translation, and copyright are visible.

## Cultural and language scope

Assess Indian, regional, oral/written tradition, language, and translation coverage. Check for false equivalence, Western default assumptions, unsupported absence claims, and translation-dependent claims attributed to the wrong creator or text.

## Screenwriting and adaptation

Where relevant, verify the package covers writing-level screenplay/adaptation mechanics and separates them from acting, direction, editing, design, music, and production. Where irrelevant, verify the recorded relevance decision and functional equivalent.

## Reception integrity

Verify professional criticism is distinct from audience response; positive, negative, and mixed evidence and representativeness limits remain visible; reception is not used as proof of intent or universal effect.

## Originality, harm, and accessibility

Assess clichés, derivative imitation, cultural flattening, sensitivity issues, content warnings, navigability, plain-language support, and relevant visual, auditory, motor, cognitive, language, or technology access needs.

## Diagnostics, exercises, and revision

Verify failure symptoms map to causes and repair methods, exercises trace to claims and guidance, completion evidence is observable, accessibility alternatives preserve the skill objective, and revision does not introduce unsupported claims.

## Cross-link and contract checks

Verify filenames, internal links, canonical paths, schema-aligned field names, evidence IDs, and status labels. Record automated results separately from this independent editorial judgment.

## Findings and disposition

For each finding record a stable finding ID, severity, affected artifact/location, evidence, impact, required remediation, owner, due date, retest required, and closure evidence. Critical, high, and evidence-affecting medium findings block pass.

## Decision

Record pass or fail, rationale, unresolved non-blocking findings, evidence paths, and signature identity. Never invent approval or represent automated success as G1 approval.

## Review evidence matrix

For each rubric dimension, record the artifact path and heading, requirement, inspected source/work/claim or verification-event IDs, locator, observed result, score, finding ID, and remediation decision. A reviewer who cannot inspect the required evidence records `unverified`; that result cannot be converted to pass. Record reviewer relationship to every authored artifact and retain sealed input/output hashes. This review may report independent editorial judgment only; human gate decisions remain separate records.
