## Parents, broader terms, and hybrids

Record parent and hybrid-parent IDs, multiple parentage, and the reason for each relationship. Hybrids link to one canonical location and are not duplicated.

## Aliases and regional terminology

## Neighbour comparisons

## Disputed classifications

## Indian, regional, and tradition relationships

## Translation, screenwriting, and adaptation
