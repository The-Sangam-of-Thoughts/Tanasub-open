# Sources and Further Reading: {{preferred_label}}

## Evidence status and limits

State whether the dossier meets the evidence floor, which areas remain incomplete, access constraints, copyright boundaries, translation limitations, and how these affect publication. Do not pad counts or imply that discovery proves a claim.

## Primary works

For each work ID, annotate its creator, period role, language, region, form/medium, edition or version used, access conditions, why it was selected, mechanics analysed, and limits. Include formative, recognised, contemporary, Indian/regional, and contrasting examples as supported.

## Scholarly and historical sources

For each source ID, state the question addressed, source role, reliability, relevant claims, counterevidence, scope, edition/access, and verification status.

## Practitioner sources and interviews

Identify expertise, practice context, supported claim IDs, possible conflicts or limits, and whether the account is prescriptive, descriptive, or retrospective.

## Professional criticism and reviews

Annotate professional criticism and long-form reviews as criticism or reception evidence; do not generalize them into audience response.

## Reader and audience response

Record audience-response evidence separately, with platform/community, sampling and representativeness limitations, date, and claims it can support. Do not use it as craft authority or proof of intent.

## Indian, regional, and translation sources

Record languages, regions, traditions, translators, editions, source roles, access barriers, and unsuccessful searches. An evidence-based explanation of absence must link to search records; it is not a universal absence claim.

## Screenwriting and adaptation sources

Where relevant, annotate authorised scripts, interviews, scholarship, criticism, and reception evidence. Exclude production-only evidence unless it changes a writing-level analysis.

## Claim and cross-link index

Map each consequential claim ID to supporting and contrary source/work IDs, scope, exceptions, confidence, and report locations. Link to all mandatory analyses, close readings, comparison, reception analysis, and genre map.
